Report handling protocol

  • Review the reported material and relevant context before taking action.
  • Keep reporter and subject information confidential.
  • Do not make public accusations or disclose private case details.
  • Escalate threats, serious harassment, safety concerns and complex legal complaints.
  • Document decisions and reasons in the authorised moderation system.
  • Do not suspend accounts or delete content unless your permissions explicitly allow it.
