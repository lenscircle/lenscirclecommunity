Partnership governance

Standards for professional and transparent relationships.

Written agreements

Document sponsorship terms, deliverables, duration, financial commitments and termination conditions.

Brand independence

Protect editorial independence and clearly disclose sponsored content and promotional relationships.

Data protection

Share partner contact information only with authorised personnel and for legitimate business purposes.

Performance review

Track agreed deliverables, community impact, event outcomes and relationship performance.

Role boundaries: The Outreach Director may manage approved outreach activities within their assigned department. Admin retains authority over platform-wide partnerships, financial commitments and formal agreements.
