Photography Tips

Tips curated by Lens Circle.
Simple ideas to help you see differently, compose thoughtfully, and capture stronger photographs.

TIP 01

Look for Leading Lines

Use roads, paths, fences, or natural lines to guide the viewer’s eyes toward your subject.

Lens Circle Team
TIP 02

Notice the Direction of Light

Observe where the light comes from. Side lighting can reveal texture, while backlighting can create a glow.

Lens Circle Team
TIP 03

Clean Your Lens

A quick lens wipe can reduce haze, smudges, and unwanted softness in your photos.

Lens Circle Team
TIP 04

Try a Different Perspective

Instead of always shooting at eye level, try a lower or higher angle to make the frame more interesting.

Lens Circle Team
TIP 05

Keep the Background Simple

Check what is behind your subject. Move your position if distracting objects compete for attention.

Lens Circle Team
TIP 06

Use the Rule of Thirds

Imagine a 3×3 grid and try placing your subject near one of the grid lines or intersections.

Lens Circle Team
TIP 07

Tap to Focus

On a phone, tap the main subject to help the camera focus where you want attention.

Lens Circle Team
TIP 08

Add Foreground Interest

Include an object near the camera in a landscape to create depth and a stronger sense of place.

Lens Circle Team
TIP 09

Give Your Subject Space

Leave some breathing room around a moving subject or the direction they are looking.

Lens Circle Team
TIP 10

Watch the Bright Areas

Check bright skies and light surfaces. If they lose detail, adjust exposure before taking the photo.

Lens Circle Team
TIP 11

Wait for the Moment

For street or candid photography, observe the scene and wait for people or movement to come together.

Lens Circle Team
TIP 12

Simplify the Frame

Before taking the shot, check the edges and remove anything that distracts from the main idea.
