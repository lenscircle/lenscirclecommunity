Editorial and rights standards

Essential safeguards for public photography exhibitions.

Photographer ownership

Photographers retain ownership of their original work. Exhibition display permission must be clearly defined.

Consent and attribution

Obtain required permissions for identifiable subjects and display photographer credits accurately.

Curatorial integrity

Apply consistent selection criteria and maintain a transparent review process for featured work.

Publication review

Check image quality, captions, credits, privacy and usage permissions before public release.

Role boundaries: The Editorial Director may oversee exhibitions within the assigned department. Admin retains platform-wide authority and privileged configuration access.
