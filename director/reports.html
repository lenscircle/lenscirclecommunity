Report handling workflow

Standard process for reviewing departmental reports

01
Receive

Receive a report from an authorised moderator or department workflow.

02
Review

Examine relevant information and identify the issue accurately.

03
Decide or escalate

Handle matters within your authority or refer them to Admin.

04
Document

Record the outcome, action taken and relevant follow-up.

Report handling boundaries

  • Access only reports belonging to your authorised department.
  • Keep sensitive reports confidential.
  • Do not disclose reporter identities unnecessarily.
  • Escalate serious safety, security and legal concerns to Admin.
  • Do not independently change global account permissions.
  • Maintain accurate records of decisions and actions.
  • Never alter or delete report evidence to conceal an incident.
