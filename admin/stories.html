Editorial workflow

Recommended process for publishing a Lens Circle story.

01

Proposal

Define the story idea, intended audience, author and editorial purpose.

02

Drafting

Prepare the article, photography, captions, credits and supporting material.

03

Review

Review accuracy, originality, writing quality, permissions and presentation.

04

Publication

Approve the final article and publish it through the public editorial platform.

Publishing standards

Quality and integrity requirements for editorial content.

Originality

Publish original work or appropriately licensed material. Credit authors and photographers clearly.

Accuracy

Verify factual claims, quotations, technical information and references before publication.

Image permissions

Obtain permission for photographs and ensure that image usage rights are clearly documented.

Editorial accountability

Maintain review history, corrections and publication decisions. Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Role boundaries: The Editorial Director oversees editorial operations within the assigned department. Admin retains global publishing policy, privileged access and platform-wide authority.
