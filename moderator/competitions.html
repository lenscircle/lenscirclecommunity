Competition lifecycle

The proposed process for running a competition from announcement through final publication.

01
Preparation

Define category, theme, eligibility, deadlines and judging criteria.

02
Registration

Publish competition information and accept participant entries.

03
Evaluation

Review eligibility and coordinate authorised judging.

04
Results

Verify outcomes and publish approved results and selected photographs.

Moderator responsibilities

  • Review entries according to published competition rules.
  • Maintain participant privacy and protect submitted photographs.
  • Identify duplicate, incomplete or potentially ineligible entries.
  • Escalate judging disputes and serious rule violations to administrators.
  • Never change scores or publish results without the required authorisation.
  • Keep competition decisions consistent, documented and fair.
