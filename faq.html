Frequently Asked Questions

New to Lens Circle? Find answers about joining the community, sharing your work, and taking part in our activities.

What is Lens Circle?
Lens Circle is a photography community where photographers and editors can share their work, learn from one another, participate in activities, and connect with other creators.
How can I join Lens Circle?
Follow Lens Circle on Instagram and check our community posts for joining instructions, events, and opportunities to participate.
Can phone photographers participate?
Yes! Phone and camera photographers are welcome. We value creativity, composition, and storytelling—not just the equipment used.
How can I submit a photo for a feature?
Follow the submission instructions shared in the relevant Lens Circle post or announcement. Include your correct Instagram handle so we can credit your work.
How are Photo of the Day and Photo of the Week selected?
Selection and voting details are shared in the relevant announcement. Please check each post for its deadline, voting process, and results.
How can I participate in contests and collaborations?
Watch our Instagram posts and community announcements for themes, rules, submission details, and deadlines. Follow the instructions provided for each activity.
Can I share photography or editing tips?
Yes. You can share useful tips, tutorials, or editing knowledge through the submission method announced by Lens Circle.
How do I get my work credited?
Always provide your correct Instagram handle when submitting work. Lens Circle aims to credit photographers and editors when their work is featured.
Where can I contact the Lens Circle team?
You can contact us through our Instagram page .

Still have a question?

Send us a message and the Lens Circle team will help you.
