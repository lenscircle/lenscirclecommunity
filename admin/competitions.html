Competition lifecycle

Recommended operational workflow for every competition.

01

Planning

Define the theme, category, eligibility, timeline and judging criteria.

02

Publication

Publish official rules, announce the competition and open submissions.

03

Judging

Review eligible entries through a transparent and documented process.

04

Results

Approve winners, publish results and archive the completed competition.

Governance & quality standards

Principles for credible and fair photography competitions.

Fair judging

Use published judging criteria, consistent evaluation procedures and conflict-of-interest safeguards.

Image authenticity

Establish clear rules for editing, manipulation, AI-generated imagery and original photographic work.

Participant rights

Clearly explain image ownership, permitted display rights, consent requirements and privacy protections.

Results integrity

Keep judging records, approval history and result publication decisions properly documented.

Role boundaries: Competition Directors should manage their assigned competition department. Admin retains platform-wide authority, including department assignments, global policy and privileged access.
