Editing Tips

Tips curated by Lens Circle.
Explore simple editing techniques and the software you can use to try them.

TIP 01

Start with Exposure

Adjust brightness first. Keep important details visible in both shadows and highlights.

Try: Snapseed · Lightroom
Lens Circle Team
TIP 02

Protect the Highlights

Lower highlights when bright skies or light areas lose detail. Avoid making the whole image too dark.

Try: Snapseed · Lightroom
Lens Circle Team
TIP 03

Use Contrast Carefully

A little contrast adds depth. Too much can hide shadow detail and make the image look harsh.

Try: Snapseed · Lightroom
Lens Circle Team
TIP 04

Keep Colours Natural

Increase saturation gently. If colours look artificial, reduce it or adjust vibrance instead.

Try: Lightroom · Snapseed
Lens Circle Team
TIP 05

Correct White Balance

Use temperature and tint to remove unwanted colour casts and make whites look believable.

Try: Snapseed · Lightroom · Photoshop
Lens Circle Team
TIP 06

Crop with Purpose

Straighten the image and remove edge distractions while keeping the subject’s important space.

Try: Snapseed · Lightroom
Lens Circle Team
TIP 07

Sharpen Selectively

Apply sharpening lightly, especially to the subject. Too much can create unwanted edges.

Try: Snapseed · Lightroom · Photoshop
Lens Circle Team
TIP 08

Reduce Noise Gently

Noise reduction can smooth a dark photo, but too much may remove natural texture and detail.

Try: Lightroom · Photoshop
Lens Circle Team
TIP 09

Make Local Adjustments

Brighten or darken specific areas to guide attention instead of changing the entire photograph.

Try: Snapseed · Lightroom · Photoshop
Lens Circle Team
TIP 10

Try a Subtle Vignette

A gentle vignette can draw attention toward the subject. Keep it subtle and natural.

Try: Snapseed · Lightroom
Lens Circle Team
TIP 11

Compare Before and After

Compare your edit with the original. If the edit distracts from the photo, simplify it.

Try: Any editing app
Lens Circle Team
TIP 12

Build a Consistent Look

For a carousel, keep exposure and colour treatment consistent so the photos feel connected.

Try: Lightroom · Snapseed
