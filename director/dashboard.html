Team leadership

Directors are responsible for the moderators assigned to their own department. The following management capabilities will be connected to the secure backend.

Appoint moderators

Add approved members to your department as moderators through an authorised workflow.

Remove moderators

Remove moderators from your department when operationally necessary.

Supervise performance

Review assigned tasks, work progress and departmental responsibilities.

Director responsibilities

  • Manage only your assigned department and its moderators.
  • Assign work fairly and communicate expectations clearly.
  • Document important team decisions and operational actions.
  • Escalate sensitive matters and cross-department issues to Admin.
  • Respect member privacy and protect submitted photographs.
  • Never grant yourself or another person higher-level access.
