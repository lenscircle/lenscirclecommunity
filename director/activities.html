Programme coordination

How Directors will oversee activity operations

Planning

Define the theme, purpose, participation rules and timeline of each activity.

Team allocation

Assign authorised department moderators to operational responsibilities.

Member participation

Coordinate announcements, entry collection and participant communication.

Review and voting

Supervise the review process and ensure fair, transparent participation.

Publication

Coordinate approved results, highlights and community announcements.

Evaluation

Review participation and document improvements for future activities.

Activity management principles

  • Publish clear themes, schedules and participation guidelines.
  • Maintain fair and transparent voting procedures.
  • Respect members' ownership and copyright of photographs.
  • Keep participant information and unpublished entries private.
  • Coordinate moderators within your authorised department.
  • Escalate cross-department decisions to Admin when necessary.
