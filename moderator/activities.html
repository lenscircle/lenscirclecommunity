Activity workflow

A proposed operational sequence for activities managed through the Lens Circle platform.

01
Plan

Define the theme, format, schedule and rules.

02
Publish

Release the announcement and invite participation.

03
Review

Check submissions and coordinate eligible entries.

04
Conclude

Publish results and archive the completed activity.

Activity management principles

  • Publish clear rules and deadlines before opening participation.
  • Maintain fair treatment of all participating photographers.
  • Keep submission and voting periods consistent with published announcements.
  • Do not manipulate votes or alter results without proper authorisation.
  • Escalate disputes and exceptional cases to the administrator.
