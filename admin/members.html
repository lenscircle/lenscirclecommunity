Account administration principles

Standards for responsible membership management.

Account protection

Passwords and authentication credentials remain protected by Supabase Auth. Admin cannot view member passwords.

Fair moderation

Account restrictions should be based on documented policy violations and appropriate review procedures.

Audit trail

Important actions, including suspensions, restrictions and reinstatements, must have a recorded reason and actor.

Implementation requirement: Member search, account suspension, reinstatement, deletion requests and moderation history are not active yet. These require a protected database design, explicit permissions and audit logging. Never delete a member's Auth record directly from frontend JavaScript.
