Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Lens Circle intends to collect, use, protect and manage information when people visit the website or participate in the community.

Important notice: This is the initial privacy-policy draft for the planned Lens Circle platform. The final policy must be checked against the actual website features, Supabase configuration, analytics tools, data flows and applicable legal requirements before member registration is enabled.
01 / Introduction

Overview

Lens Circle is a photography community designed to connect photographers, creators and photography enthusiasts through discovery, activities, competitions, exhibitions and editorial content.

This policy explains how information may be handled when you use Lens Circle's website and services.

In this policy, "Lens Circle", "we", "our" and "us" refer to the Lens Circle platform and its operators. "You" refers to a visitor, member or other person interacting with the platform.

02 / Data collection

Information we may collect

Account information

If account registration is introduced, we may collect information such as:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Authentication and account identifiers.
  • Profile photograph, biography and optional profile details.
  • Account preferences and settings.

Community information

  • Photographs, titles, descriptions and captions submitted by members.
  • Competition and activity entries.
  • Votes, comments, bookmarks and participation records, where those features are enabled.
  • Messages, reports and support requests submitted through platform features.

Technical information

Depending on hosting, security and analytics configuration, limited technical information may be processed, such as browser type, device information, approximate usage data, IP address and security logs.

Information you choose not to provide

Some features may require certain information to function. Optional profile details should be clearly identified. You should not provide sensitive personal information unless a specific feature genuinely requires it and appropriate safeguards are in place.

03 / Creator rights

Photographs and creative content

Your photographs are an important part of Lens Circle. We recognise that creators should retain ownership of their original work.

  • You retain applicable rights in photographs and other original content you create.
  • Content may be displayed to operate the feature for which it was submitted.
  • Public submissions may be visible to other visitors if the relevant feature is designed as public.
  • Private account information should not be displayed publicly unless you choose to share it or it is necessary and lawful.
  • Additional permission may be required for promotional, advertising or commercial reuse beyond ordinary platform display.

Before uploading, review the visibility settings and submission terms associated with the relevant feature. Do not upload another person's photograph without appropriate permission.

04 / Purpose

How information may be used

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage member accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect account access.
  • Display member profiles and authorised photography submissions.
  • Operate competitions, exhibitions, voting and community activities.
  • Respond to support requests and reports.
  • Moderate content and enforce community guidelines.
  • Maintain website performance and security.
  • Improve features and understand aggregate platform usage.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We should collect and use only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

05 / Disclosure

When information may be shared

We do not intend to sell members' personal information. Information may be shared only where appropriate, such as:

  • With service providers needed to operate the platform.
  • With authorised moderators or administrators where necessary for their duties.
  • When you choose to publish a profile or photograph publicly.
  • When required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • When necessary to investigate abuse, fraud or security incidents.
  • With your consent for a specific purpose.

Public photographs, comments and creator profiles may be visible to people outside Lens Circle if the relevant feature is publicly accessible.

06 / Platform infrastructure

Hosting and technology providers

The planned platform uses GitHub Pages for website hosting and Supabase for backend services such as authentication, database and file storage.

These providers may process technical or account information as necessary to provide their services, subject to their own terms, privacy policies and applicable configuration.

The final version of this policy should identify the actual providers, enabled services, relevant data locations and applicable contractual arrangements after implementation is complete.

07 / Protection

Data security

We intend to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Planned safeguards include:

  • Role-based access controls.
  • Database access policies and Row Level Security.
  • Secure authentication practices.
  • Restricted administrative access.
  • Appropriate file-storage permissions.
  • Security monitoring and audit records where implemented.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Members should use strong, unique passwords and protect their account credentials.

08 / Storage duration

Data retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods will depend on the type of information, the feature involved, security requirements and legal obligations. The final platform should define retention and deletion procedures for accounts, submissions, moderation records and backups.

09 / Your choices

Your choices and privacy rights

Subject to applicable law and verification requirements, you may be able to:

  • Request access to personal information associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account or eligible information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing where applicable.
  • Raise concerns about how your information is handled.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate security, dispute-resolution or legal reasons. Deletion from public display may not immediately remove every backup or legally required record.

We will publish a verified privacy contact and a practical request process before public registration begins.

10 / Website technologies

Cookies and analytics

The current static website may use essential browser functionality and third-party resources such as web fonts and remote images.

Additional cookies, analytics tools or similar technologies may be introduced in the future. If introduced, their purpose, operation and relevant choices should be explained here and in any required consent interface.

11 / Younger users

Children's privacy

Lens Circle is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Any future age requirements, parental consent procedures and safeguards for younger users must be defined before registration is opened to them.

12 / Global community

International visitors

Lens Circle aims to serve a global photography community. Depending on the providers and their infrastructure, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required, appropriate safeguards and disclosures should be implemented for international data transfers.

13 / Updates

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Lens Circle introduces new features, changes its technology providers or responds to legal and operational requirements.

Material changes should be communicated through appropriate website notices or other suitable communication channels.

14 / Contact

Questions about privacy?

For privacy-related questions or requests, use the Lens Circle contact page.

A verified privacy contact address and formal data-request procedure must be added before public launch.
