Competition lifecycle

Standard operational workflow

01
Plan

Define the theme, categories, eligibility and competition rules.

02
Announce

Publish the competition and communicate deadlines and participation requirements.

03
Collect entries

Coordinate submissions and verify compliance with published rules.

04
Judge

Coordinate impartial judging and maintain a documented review process.

05
Publish results

Verify approved results and coordinate winner announcements.

06
Archive

Preserve competition records and approved highlights for future reference.

Judging standards

Principles for trustworthy competition operations

A
Originality

Respect original creative work and published entry requirements.

B
Technical quality

Consider focus, exposure, composition and appropriate technique.

C
Creative vision

Recognise storytelling, interpretation and originality of approach.

D
Fair evaluation

Apply consistent criteria and avoid conflicts of interest.

Director responsibilities

  • Coordinate competition operations within your assigned department.
  • Ensure competition rules are communicated before entries open.
  • Maintain participant privacy and protect unpublished entries.
  • Ensure judging processes are fair and documented.
  • Escalate serious disputes, disqualifications and exceptional cases to Admin.
  • Never alter judging outcomes for personal or preferential reasons.
