Assignment management rules

  • Assign work only to moderators in your authorised department.
  • Every assignment should have a clear objective and expected outcome.
  • Set reasonable deadlines and priorities.
  • Do not assign platform-wide administrative or security responsibilities.
  • Record important changes and completion decisions.
  • Escalate work that requires another department or Admin approval.
