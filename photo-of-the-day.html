Before you submit

Keep it original. Keep it respectful.

A few basic expectations help make the activity fair and enjoyable for everyone.

Submission guidelines

  • Submit your own original photograph.
  • Follow the announced daily theme.
  • Send your entry before 12:00 PM IST.
  • Include your Instagram username or creator credit.
  • Do not submit stolen or unauthorised photographs.
  • Follow any additional instructions in the daily announcement.

Community voting

  • Voting takes place until 8:00 PM IST.
  • Appreciate photographs fairly and respectfully.
  • Do not use fake accounts or automated voting.
  • Do not pressure others to vote for a particular entry.
  • Respect the published result and activity rules.
  • Follow the official announcement for result updates.
