Data and privacy standards

Principles governing the platform analytics system.

Data accuracy

Metrics must originate from reliable records and clearly defined measurement rules.

Privacy protection

Avoid exposing personal information in aggregate dashboards and reports.

Access control

Restrict platform-wide analytics to authorised Admin accounts.

Auditability

Maintain reliable reporting periods, calculation definitions and data provenance.

Meaningful metrics

Measure meaningful participation and community quality rather than vanity numbers alone.

Responsible reporting

Use aggregated information and avoid unnecessary member-level surveillance.

Implementation requirement: Analytics charts, exports and reports require verified source tables, suitable database views or protected aggregation functions. Sensitive data must never be exposed through unrestricted client queries.
