Fairness and originality

Every creator deserves a fair chance.

Lens Circle aims to build a respectful and transparent competition environment where creativity and originality matter.


Read community guidelines →
  • 01 Submit photographs that you created or have the necessary rights and permission to enter.
  • 02 Follow the specific theme, format and deadline announced for each competition.
  • 03 Respect other participants and avoid plagiarism, impersonation or misleading submissions.
  • 04 Follow the published judging and voting rules for each competition.
  • 05 Review the individual competition terms before submitting an entry.
