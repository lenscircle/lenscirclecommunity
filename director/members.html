Member management principles

  • Access only member information needed for departmental responsibilities.
  • Respect member privacy and photography ownership.
  • Do not access private information without a legitimate authorised purpose.
  • Do not suspend or delete member accounts through this directory.
  • Escalate serious complaints and account-related matters to Admin.
  • Never export or share member data without authorisation.
