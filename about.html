About Lens Circle

The world, through your lens.

We are building an international photography community where creativity connects people, every perspective has value and visual stories find an audience.

Our story

More than a place to share photographs.

Lens Circle is being built around a simple belief: photography is a powerful way to observe, interpret and connect with the world.

A photograph can preserve a moment, communicate an emotion, reveal a perspective or tell a story that words cannot fully express.

We want to create a space where photographers can discover one another, participate in creative activities, showcase their work and continue developing their craft.

Our ambition is to grow into an international photography society that celebrates both the art of photography and the people behind it.

Our purpose

What we stand for.

Our mission

Connect through creativity.

To make photography more accessible, collaborative and rewarding by connecting photographers, encouraging creative participation and providing opportunities to share and showcase visual work.

Our vision

A world connected through photography.

To build a globally recognised photography society and creative platform where photographers from different backgrounds can discover, learn, participate and be recognised.

Our principles

The values behind the circle.

These principles guide how we build the community and how we want members to experience it.

01

Creativity

We encourage experimentation, imagination and individual photographic expression.

02

Inclusivity

Photography should be accessible to everyone, regardless of experience, equipment or location.

03

Respect

We value creators, their work, their rights and the different perspectives they bring.

04

Discovery

We create opportunities to explore new styles, photographers, subjects and ideas.

05

Recognition

We believe meaningful creative work deserves visibility and appropriate attribution.

06

Community

We believe photography becomes more meaningful when people learn, participate and grow together.
