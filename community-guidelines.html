Community Guidelines

Lens Circle is a space for photographers and editors to share their work, learn from one another, and build meaningful creative connections. These guidelines help keep the community welcoming and respectful.

1. Be respectful

Treat every member with kindness. Give constructive feedback that helps photographers improve, and avoid insults, harassment, or personal attacks.

2. Share work you have permission to share

Submit photographs and edits that you created, or that you have clear permission to share. Do not claim another person's work as your own.

3. Credit creators properly

When sharing or featuring someone else's work with permission, clearly credit the photographer or editor. Do not remove watermarks or creator credits without permission.

4. Keep submissions relevant

Follow the instructions for each feature, event, or contest, including its theme, format, deadline, and submission method. Submissions that do not follow the stated requirements may not be included.

5. Respect privacy and safety

Do not share private information, images of people in sensitive situations, or content that could put someone at risk. Get appropriate permission before posting identifiable people, especially children.

6. No spam or misleading activity

Avoid repetitive promotions, unsolicited messages, fake engagement, and misleading claims about your work or identity. Keep community discussions focused on photography, editing, and related creative topics.

7. Features and contests

Being featured or selected is not guaranteed. Follow the specific rules and selection process announced for each activity. Lens Circle may decline or remove submissions that violate these guidelines.
