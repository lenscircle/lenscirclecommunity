A few helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Is Lens Circle membership free?

Yes. Core community membership is free.

Can beginners join Lens Circle?

Absolutely. Photographers at different experience levels are welcome.

How can I participate in competitions?

Explore the competitions section and follow the submission instructions published for each event.

Can I propose a partnership?

Yes. Select Partnerships in the contact form and describe your proposal.

Can I contribute photography articles?

Yes. Select Editorial contributions and share your proposed topic or idea.
